About Orbit

Teacher training built around your life

Orbit Teaching Academy is a teacher-training and examination academy based in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. We help people train to teach English to adults and take that training into classrooms around the world.

Read more about CELTA and Orbit

CELTA (Certificate in English Language Teaching to Adults) is awarded by Cambridge Assessment English, part of the University of Cambridge. Unlike many online qualifications, it requires observed, assessed teaching practice with real adult learners, which is why employers value it.

Our online courses let you complete that training from home, on a full-time, part-time or Saturday schedule.

How It Works

How the online CELTA works

CELTA courses involve a minimum of 120 contact hours of tuition and assessed teaching practice.

Apply

Fees and Admission

Course fees and requirements

Tuition

$2,400 USD per candidate
  • Full CELTA training
  • Observed teaching practice and tutor feedback
  • Course materials and resources

A non-refundable $200 registration fee secures your place and is deducted from tuition. The remaining $2,200 is due five weeks before the course begins. We do not ask for payment until you have completed the application process and been offered a place. Registration terms, including any option to move the fee to a later course, are confirmed when you are offered a place.

Entry requirements

  • Age 18 or older
  • English at C1 level or higher
  • High school diploma or equivalent
  • No prior teaching experience needed
  • A degree is not required for the CELTA itself (some employers and visa programmes require one)

Final eligibility is confirmed through the application process: the application form, a pre-interview writing task and a conversation with a course advisor.

FAQ

Online CELTA questions, answered

What is the difference between requesting information and applying?

Requesting information is a quick enquiry with no commitment: tell us what you are interested in and we will reply by email. Starting your CELTA application is the first step toward a place on a course. It is the full application form, followed by a writing task and a conversation with a course advisor.

What are the entry requirements for the online CELTA?

The proposed requirements are: age 18 or older, English at C1 level or higher, and a high school diploma or equivalent. No teaching experience is needed, and a university degree is not required for the CELTA itself, although some employers and visa programmes do require one. Final eligibility is confirmed through the application process.

How much does the online CELTA cost?

Tuition is $2,400 USD per candidate for any course format. A non-refundable $200 registration fee secures your place and is deducted from tuition, and the remaining $2,200 is due five weeks before the course begins. We do not ask for payment until you have completed the application process and been offered a place.

What are the 2027 online CELTA dates?

We are proposing 11 full-time courses (four instructional weeks each), four part-time courses (10 weeks each) and three Saturday courses (16 weeks each) across 2027. The November full-time course is four weeks of intensive training delivered across five calendar weeks, with a Thanksgiving break. All dates are proposed and will be confirmed before enrolment.

Which online CELTA format should I choose?

Full-time is the fastest route and suits people who can commit their whole day for several weeks. Part-time spreads the course over 10 weeks. The Saturday course runs over 16 weeks and is designed for people who need to keep working. We will confirm the full timetable for each format before enrolment.

Are Saturday sessions the whole course?

No. Saturday sessions run 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM, but they alone do not meet the CELTA minimum of 120 contact hours. Additional supervised sessions will be included in the finalised Saturday timetable, and we will confirm them before enrolment.

Is there a break in the November full-time course?

Yes. The course starting November 15, 2027 is four weeks of intensive training delivered across five calendar weeks, with a Thanksgiving break. There are no classes November 22 to 26, 2027, and classes resume November 29.

Can I work while taking the course?

Full-time courses are intensive, and working alongside them is not recommended. The part-time and Saturday formats are intended to be more manageable alongside a job, but they still need consistent study and assignment time outside class.

Does the online CELTA include teaching practice?

CELTA includes observed, assessed teaching practice with real adult learners. How teaching practice is arranged for each online course will be confirmed before enrolment.

What time zone are classes in?

Classes run on Eastern Time. The full daily timetable for each format will be published before enrolment.

What happens after I apply?

After you submit the application, you will be asked to complete a pre-interview writing task and then have a conversation with a course advisor to confirm that CELTA and your chosen format are right for you. If you are offered a place, you secure it with the $200 registration fee.

Is career support offered after the course?

We offer career guidance to graduates, such as help with applications and interview preparation, and access to the Orbit alumni community. The details of this support are confirmed at enrolment.

What if I need to change my start date?

Registration terms, including whether a registration fee can be moved to a later course, are confirmed when you are offered a place. Speak to your course advisor if your plans change.
