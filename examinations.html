Cambridge University Examinations

Internationally recognised
English qualifications

We offer the full suite of Cambridge Assessment English examinations — from foundational to proficiency level. Each qualification is globally recognised and mapped to the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR).

A2 · CEFR
KET
Key English Test (A2 Key)
The Cambridge A2 Key qualification proves that a candidate can communicate in simple situations and demonstrates basic English ability in reading, writing, listening, and speaking.
Best for: Beginner learners building foundational English skills
B1 · CEFR
PET
Preliminary English Test (B1 Preliminary)
The B1 Preliminary qualification demonstrates intermediate English skills — suitable for those who can handle straightforward written and spoken communications in familiar situations.
Best for: Intermediate learners preparing for work or study abroad
B2 · CEFR
FCE
First Certificate in English (B2 First)
One of Cambridge's most widely recognised qualifications. B2 First proves upper-intermediate English ability and is accepted by thousands of universities and employers worldwide as proof of ability to study or work in English.
Best for: University entry, professional employment, and study programmes
C1 · CEFR
CAE
Certificate in Advanced English (C1 Advanced)
The C1 Advanced qualification demonstrates high-level English ability for demanding academic and professional contexts. Widely accepted by top universities and multinational employers as proof of advanced English proficiency.
Best for: Elite university admission, senior professional roles, postgraduate study
C2 · CEFR
CPE
Certificate of Proficiency in English (C2 Proficiency)
The highest level Cambridge qualification — C2 Proficiency demonstrates mastery of English at the very highest level, equivalent to that of a highly educated native speaker. The benchmark qualification for complete English fluency.
Best for: The highest-level academic and professional contexts worldwide
Business · CEFR
BEC
Business English Certificate (Preliminary, Vantage, Higher)
The Cambridge Business English Certificates are internationally recognised qualifications that prove ability to communicate in English in business and professional settings — available at B1, B2, and C1 levels.
Best for: Business professionals, corporate teams, and commercial environments
